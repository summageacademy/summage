We Will Pay For Your IELTS Exam

Our New Program to Support Dedicated Students

Preparing for the IELTS exam requires a significant amount of time, energy, and focus. Aside from the hours spent studying vocabulary and practicing essays, the registration fee itself can be a major financial commitment for many families and students. We want to remove this financial obstacle so you can focus entirely on reaching your target score.

Our main goal at Summage Academy is to make quality education more accessible. To help our community move forward, we are starting a seasonal program where we fully cover the official IELTS exam fee for our most dedicated learners.

How the Selection Works

Every season, our team selects a group of students from our platform and covers their entire exam cost. The selection process is based entirely on your commitment and effort rather than an entrance test. You do not need to be a perfect English speaker from day one to qualify.

To be considered for the program, your primary requirement is to study with us regularly and stay active on our platform. We look closely at daily practice consistency and progress.

Why We Value Activity

Consistency is the single most important factor when you learn a new language. By tracking daily study streaks, completed lessons, and practice tests, our system identifies students who are genuinely putting in the effort to improve their skills. We want to reward learners who show up every day and take their preparation seriously.

Using our platform features, practicing with real questions, and completing assignments all count toward your overall activity status. This approach allows every student an equal opportunity to get their exam paid for, regardless of their starting level.

Registration Opening Soon

Our team is currently completing the final setup for the upcoming season, and registration for this initiative will open very soon. You can prepare by creating an account early and building a steady habit of daily practice on the platform.

An early start gives you a better opportunity to establish a strong activity history before the selection period begins. You can set up your profile right now to explore our learning tools and start practicing: Sign Up and Start Learning, Level Up Your English with Summage Academy!

A Few Simple Guidelines

Please make sure you complete your profile information accurately so we can contact you easily if you are chosen. Try to utilize different sections of the platform, including vocabulary building and speaking practice, to show well rounded engagement.

We look forward to seeing your progress on the platform and helping you achieve your academic goals without financial worry.
