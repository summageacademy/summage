Linking Words & Cohesion

Advanced connectors for better coherence

Hey! One of the biggest differences between a Band 6 and Band 7.5+ essay is cohesion which is how smoothly your ideas connect together. Many students use linking words, but they either use them wrongly, repeat the same ones or overuse them, which actually hurts their score. In this lesson, I'll show you how to use linking words naturally and effectively to make your writing flow beautifully.

What is Cohesion and Why Does It Matter?

Cohesion refers to how well your sentences and paragraphs connect to each other. Good cohesion makes your essay easy to follow and shows the examiner that you can organize ideas logically. It’s 25% of your Task 2 score, so getting this right is very important.

Remember: It's mostly about using the right ones naturally. Not about using as many linking words as possible.

Types of Linking Words You Should Know

Adding Ideas:
Furthermore, • Moreover, • In addition, • Additionally, • Not only... but also...
Contrasting Ideas:
However, • On the other hand, • In contrast, • Nevertheless, • Although, • While
Giving Examples:
For example, • For instance, • Such as, • A good example of this is...
Showing Cause & Effect:
Therefore, • As a result, • Consequently, • This is because..., • Due to
Sequencing:
First of all, • Secondly, • Finally, • To begin with, • Last but not least

Good vs Bad Use of Linking Words

Weak Example (Overused & Mechanical):

First of all, social media is bad. Moreover, it wastes time. Furthermore, it causes anxiety. In addition, it is addictive.

Strong Example (Natural & Smooth):

One of the main drawbacks of social media is its negative impact on mental health. Many young people compare themselves to unrealistic images online, which often leads to anxiety and low self-esteem. As a result, they may feel constantly dissatisfied with their own lives.

Common Mistakes Students Make

  • Starting almost every sentence with a linking word (First, Moreover, However...)
  • Using the same linking words repeatedly (especially "and", "but", "so")
  • Using advanced linking words incorrectly (e.g. "Nevertheless" when it doesn’t fit)
  • Putting linking words in the wrong place in the sentence
  • Forcing linking words where they're not needed
  • Using informal linkers like "plus", "anyway", or "by the way"

Practical Tips for Better Cohesion

  1. Don't rely only on linking words. Use pronouns (it, this, they), repetition of key words and synonyms for natural flow
  2. Plan your two main ideas clearly before writing so the essay flows logically
  3. Use a variety of linking methods, not just obvious words like "however"
  4. Read your essay aloud. If it sounds smooth and natural, you're on the right track
  5. Practice using 8-12 different linking words/phrases across your essay
  6. Ask yourself: “Does this sentence clearly connect to the previous one?”

Good cohesion is what makes your essay feel professional and well-organized. It's all about making your ideas easy to follow. The more you practice, the more natural your writing will become.
